# IEEE AI-use policy and verifiable project contribution record

Checked on 2026-09-28 against official publisher pages. This is a draft disclosure aid, not an authorship decision or submission authorization.

## Current publisher requirements

[IEEE Author Center, Submission and Peer Review Policies, “Use by Authors”](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-journal-author/publishing-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/submission-and-peer-review-policies/) says AI-generated article content, including text, figures, images or code, belongs in the article's **Acknowledgments**. It asks authors to identify the AI system, the affected article sections, and the level of generation. It treats grammar/editing-only use separately; disclosure for those cases is recommended. [IEEE Open's author guidance](https://open.ieee.org/author-guidelines-for-artificial-intelligence-ai-generated-text/) restates the content scope and section/extent rule. [IEEE Access submission guidelines, item 2](https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/submission-guidelines/) additionally say article sections with AI-generated text should cite the system; the venue-specific implementation needs human review.

The current PR #90 shared manuscript now has a draft Acknowledgments section after the Conclusion at `papers/paper3_reviewer_remediation/manuscript/shared/body.tex` (lines 147–148 at this audit). It names OpenAI Codex for code/analysis tools, manuscript drafting/editing, and figure/table production, and explicitly leaves the Claude-assisted critique's version and extent for human confirmation. **The location requirement is addressed in source, but the disclosure is not final**: the authors must verify which manuscript sections were affected, the degree of AI-generated content, any separate ChatGPT use, the precise Claude role, and venue-specific inline-citation requirements. In the final Level-A reporting build, text extraction and rendered-page visual inspection found a legible Acknowledgments on page 8 of both TMLCN and IEEE Access main PDFs (their exact hashes are recorded in `reproducibility_levels.md`). No submission was made.
