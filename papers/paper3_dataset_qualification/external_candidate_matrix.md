## First-pass outcome

Seventeen candidates were considered. Five advance to deeper evidence review: WiSig, OSU LoRa RFFP, OPERAnet, Antwerp LPWAN localization, and the POWDER Data Commons. Oxford Radar RobotCar is metadata-rich but is retained as a benchmark for what valid temporal provenance looks like, not as an RF spectrum-situation candidate.

The matrix does not equate SigMF compliance with valid temporal context. A SigMF timestamp or sample index still fails if the recording is target-pure or if reset/gap semantics are absent.
