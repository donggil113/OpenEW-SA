# Top-Five Evidence: POWDER Data Commons

- **Official source:** University of Utah [POWDER Data Commons](https://powderwireless.net/data).
- **Scope warning:** this is a collection portal, not one uniform dataset. Every candidate item needs an independent licence/schema decision.
- **Verified promising items:** ongoing sub-6 GHz scans from available POWDER nodes since August 2020; a 5.3 GiB massive-MIMO site survey with five candidate rooftop receivers; a 2020 RF-fingerprinting release; and newer timestamped spectrum/handover tables with cell IDs, location, and RSRP.
- **Potential relations:** physical node/site, serving/neighbor cell, campaign, and timestamp. They are only candidates until item-level schemas show target neutrality and coverage.
- **Proxy risk:** endpoint/transmitter location may be the prediction target in localization collections; cell/event fields may encode labels; RF-fingerprinting transmitter identity is forbidden.
- **Temporal:** the ongoing scan archive and newer tabular records appear ordered/timestamped, but session/reset/gap semantics were not verified.
- **Licence/access:** official download links exist; a common payload licence is not stated on the portal. Status UNRESOLVED.
- **Task fit:** spectrum scanning and interference collections are the closest semantic fit to situation assessment, but no single ready item passed all gates.
- **Verdict:** MAYBE; conduct an item-level metadata/licence audit before any download or converter.
