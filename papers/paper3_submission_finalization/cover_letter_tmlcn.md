# TMLCN cover letter — human approval required; not sent

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “Unlabeled Receiver Adaptation for RF
Fingerprinting Under Unseen-Receiver Shift: An Information-Matched Comparison
on WiSig” for IEEE Transactions on Machine Learning in Communications and
Networking, subject to final approval by its human authors.

The study compares selected source-only and unlabeled receiver-adaptation
procedures on a six-transmitter WiSig task under 32 physical-receiver
leave-one-receiver-out evaluations. Deployable methods receive the same bounded,
label-free target-receiver support information, separate from evaluation queries
by packet ID. Receiver-level inference, information-access accounting, and
probability-quality analysis make both the strengths and limitations of the
evaluated procedures visible. T3A improves on the source-only reference in the
frozen receiver-level analysis; an attentive receiver-context model does not
show a meaningful improvement over that reference. A simple embedding-statistics
control and post-hoc support-composition diagnostics further qualify the
interpretation.

This is a bounded, single-dataset comparison, not a claim of a new superior
architecture, external validation, or physically acquired receiver-adaptation
sessions. The original WiSig RF payload is not being redistributed. We believe
the information-matched comparison and explicit negative findings are pertinent
to the journal's communications and machine-learning readership.
