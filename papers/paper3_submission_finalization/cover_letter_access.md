Dear Editor,

Please consider “Unlabeled Receiver Adaptation for RF Fingerprinting Under
Unseen-Receiver Shift: An Information-Matched Comparison on WiSig” for IEEE
Access, subject to final approval by its human authors.

This article reports an information-matched comparison of selected source-only
and unlabeled receiver-adaptation methods under 32 held-out physical receivers.
The analysis identifies a strong T3A result, a neutral attentive-context result,
and a simple embedding-standardization control. It also reports receiver-level
heterogeneity, bounded support-budget behavior, probability-quality outcomes,
and label-dependent post-hoc support-composition stress tests. The diagnostic
oracle conditions are not presented as deployable support-selection methods.

The contribution is a transparent benchmark and analysis of receiver adaptation
on WiSig, not a state-of-the-art or universal superiority claim. The evidence
comes from one dataset, with deterministic packet-disjoint support rather than
physically acquired calibration episodes; external generality remains untested.
Source code and permitted derived summaries are subject to the authors' release
review, while the original WiSig payload is not redistributed.
