# Context-composition confound report

Status: **PRE-UNBLINDING STRUCTURE; NUMERICAL FINDINGS WITHHELD**

## Scope and estimand

This post-hoc diagnostic asks whether the receiver-level P2-minus-P0 difference varies with the composition of the frozen, label-free 128-packet receiver support bank. Support IDs were fixed before labels were opened. Labels are used here only to characterize those immutable banks and construct explicitly nondeployable oracle controls.

The inferential unit for the primary study remains the held-out receiver. Correlations are reported both at receiver × seed grain and after seed averaging within receiver; neither is a preregistered mechanism test, and attention weights are not interpreted causally.

## VERIFIED RESULT

To be populated solely from the validated external `report_evidence.json` after the one-time unblinding and all oracle diagnostics complete.

Required disclosures:

- support transmitter-count and entropy ranges;
- majority-class and same-class-presence associations with P2-minus-P0;
- full query/evaluable coverage for each oracle condition;
- same-class-excluded result;
- transmitter-pure bias toward the selected support transmitter;
- same-class-only oracle upper-bound behavior.

## INTERPRETATION

Withheld until verified results exist. The report will state directly whether the natural-support effect survives same-class exclusion and whether support composition plausibly explains part or all of the PR #84 effect.
