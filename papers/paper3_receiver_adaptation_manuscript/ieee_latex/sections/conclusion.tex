\section{Conclusion}
On the frozen WiSig receiver-LOSO protocol, T3A improves transmitter recognition over source-only ERM using the same bounded unlabeled support available to context methods. Attentive receiver-context conditioning is sensitive to matched support but provides no meaningful mean benefit over P0 and is inferior to T3A. This supports a receiver-adaptation benchmark framing, not P2 superiority. Support dependence, probability-calibration tradeoffs, reused-dataset inference, and absent acquired episodes remain central limitations. Independent lawful data or a prospective target-neutral collection is the next required evidence. Internal manuscript review is warranted; cross-dataset and deployment-general claims are not.
