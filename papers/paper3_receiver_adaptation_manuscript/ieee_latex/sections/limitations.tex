\section{Limitations}
\textbf{Single dataset and restricted classes.} Quantitative claims concern the same six-transmitter WiSig V2 subset. The full WiSig universe and original context study are not interchangeable cohorts. There is no Shen result, prospective scientific dataset, or cross-dataset generality claim.

\textbf{Constructed support episodes.} Hashing gives reproducible label-independent separation, but acquisition did not physically create calibration episodes. WiSig captures are transmitter-specific. Packet ordering is not temporal context; representative support availability is an assumption.

\textbf{Incomplete baseline coverage.} BatchNorm-based AdaBN/Tent and the inspected Shen architecture were not faithfully compatible with the fixed GroupNorm/short-IQ contract. Architectural control is preserved at the cost of incomplete adaptation-method coverage.

\textbf{Dependence and evidence reuse.} Receiver inference avoids packet pseudoreplication but not dependence from overlapping LOSO training, shared capture design, or hardware families. Seeds are not additional receivers. Earlier WiSig evidence informed the later T3A comparison family even though subsequent execution and reporting were frozen.

\textbf{Calibration and cost measurement.} ECE depends on bins and score normalization. Lower NLL and higher ECE can coexist. Timing scopes differ and do not establish deployment latency or energy. Models were not rerun to fill missing fields.

\textbf{Release and realism.} WiSig's noncommercial ShareAlike terms constrain distribution. Code and small summaries do not automatically resolve institutional licensing. Only three receiver families are represented. Synchronized, geographically independent, or physically acquired calibration claims are unsupported.
