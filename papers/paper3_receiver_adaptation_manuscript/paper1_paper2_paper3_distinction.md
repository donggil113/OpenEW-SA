# Paper distinction and anti-fragmentation boundary

| Work | Primary question | Evidence contribution | Not claimed here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | How do RF classification benchmarks change under domain holdout? | Domain-shift benchmark and frozen protocols | Repeating random/holdout comparisons as a new method |
| Paper 2 | How do uncertainty and feature distance behave for OOD/open-set recognition? | OOD scores and evaluation | New OOD scoring, gating or score tuning |
| Paper 3 | How does bounded unlabeled receiver information alter closed-set identification on unseen receivers? | Information-matched adaptation comparison, receiver unit, support robustness and negative context finding | New T3A algorithm or P2 superiority |

The task is closed-set and the intervention is target information access, not OOD detection. Earlier OpenEW-SA negative relational results are preserved as methodological motivation, not repackaged benchmark achievements. Reusing a dataset/split alone does not establish sufficient novelty: internal reviewers must assess the adaptation question, transparent method exclusions, and single-dataset limitation. A future cross-dataset paper should integrate these results rather than publish overlapping fragments. Cite related OpenEW-SA papers only after their actual publication metadata are confirmed; no invented BibTeX entry is inserted.
