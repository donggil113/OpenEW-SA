# Artifact release plan

## Proposed releasable material, subject to repository and institutional review

Original source code, unit tests, small synthetic fixtures/templates, split definitions, immutable hashes, small receiver-level summaries, numerical provenance, IEEE LaTeX and generated aggregate vector figures. Preserve project licence notices and credit WiSig, its authors and ORBIT. Generated manuscript content is original; third-party RF payload/code is not vendored.

## Excluded

WiSig raw archive, I/Q shards, packet-level prediction archives, source target-bearing filenames/paths, model checkpoints, original annotations and any Shen payload. Opaque IDs/manifests are not automatically unrestricted; review linkage risk and derivative terms before distribution. This package exports no packet identifiers or features.

WiSig official terms are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Noncommercial local research authorization does not settle redistribution of derivatives or institutional obligations. Raw/converted payload remains external. Do not remove attribution, imply a commercial permission or infer payload terms from the paper's copyright.

Shen lawful access remains blocked. Prepared email is UNSENT. The mock converter has no authority to bypass credentials or licence ambiguity.

Before public release: human rights review, secret/path scan, author approval, source and figure attribution review, exact tagged commit and environment snapshot. A draft PR is not a data licence grant. Model reruns still require lawfully obtained datasets and the original frozen procedure.
