Dear Editors,

Please consider, after completion of the authors' internal and institutional review, “Receiver Adaptation for RF Fingerprinting Under Unseen-Receiver Shift.”

The manuscript evaluates source-only recognition, unlabeled test-time adaptation and receiver-context conditioning using a bounded, disjoint target-support protocol on WiSig. It reports receiver-level effects and a transparent post-hoc reviewer-completeness addendum. T3A is the highest-mean evaluated unlabeled reference-budget method; the learned context model does not meaningfully exceed the independent baseline. A simple feature-moment control and a stronger labeled diagnostic qualify the interpretation.

The work does not claim cross-dataset generality, physically acquired adaptation episodes, or a new superior neural architecture. We believe the information-matched evaluation and probability-quality analysis may be useful to communications/ML readers, subject to editorial assessment of the single-dataset contribution.
