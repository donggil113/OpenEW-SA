# Shen licence questions — unresolved

1. Which entity controls the specific payload and which exact licence/version applies?
2. Does DataPort hosting impose additional account/subscription/research terms?
3. Is local noncommercial processing and conversion explicitly permitted?
4. May code, splits, opaque identifiers, aggregate tables and figures be published?
5. May derived I/Q/features, pretrained weights or checkpoints be redistributed?
6. Do derivative outputs require ShareAlike, attribution, access restrictions or source disclosure?
7. Is a separate institutional agreement, approval or data-use addendum required?
8. What official citation, acknowledgements and dataset version must accompany results?
9. Is access revocable, and what retention/deletion requirements apply?
10. Can the authors resolve the prior licence conflict in a written authoritative statement?

Paper copyright is not payload permission. “Research use” in an informal description is not automatically a redistribution grant. Until controlling terms are verified and human institutional approval is recorded, the gate remains BLOCKED; no credentials or access controls are bypassed.
