# Concrete collection tiers

These are prospective design minima, not statistical power estimates.

| Tier | Physical receivers | Hardware families | Sites | Days per site | Required roles per receiver/site/day |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| SMALL | 8 | at least 3 | 2 | 1 | Separate CALIBRATION and QUERY |
| MEDIUM | 12 | at least 3 | 2 | at least 2 | Separate CALIBRATION and QUERY |
| FULL | 20 | at least 4 | 3 | at least 2 | Separate CALIBRATION and QUERY |

Minimum session counts under complete participation: SMALL 32, MEDIUM 96, FULL 240. Use the same physical receiver UUID across sites and days. Each session should contain repeated target-neutral captures with mixed transmitter activity. The mix is checked only afterward using separate annotations. Sufficient class/receiver support and a scientific split freeze remain additional requirements.

templates/<tier>_requirements.json and <tier>_campaign.json are concrete inventory/planning templates. The real templates deliberately contain invalid placeholder UTC/rate values, forcing operator completion before campaign-init. They do not assert any receiver was purchased or deployed. Synthetic templates generated by the stress harness remain explicitly distinct.

## Illustrative storage scenario, not a recommended RF operating frequency

For planning only: complex float32 I/Q (8 bytes/sample), 1 MS/s, one-second captures, ten captures per physical session, complete tier participation. SMALL: 320 captures, 2.56 GB raw, 320 receiver-seconds. MEDIUM: 960 captures, 7.68 GB raw, 960 receiver-seconds. FULL: 2,400 captures, 19.2 GB raw, 2,400 receiver-seconds. Decimal GB; simultaneous receivers reduce wall time but setup/clock/annotation overhead is additional.

The estimator conservatively reserves two raw copies, two float32 conversion passes and 25% headroom; checkpoints and operator backups beyond those copies are additional. No compression ratio is promised for noisy RF. Substitute actual sample format/rate/duration before field work.
